How we work

One ticket, one crew, one finished unit

Real-estate companies and property managers do not need another vendor to manage. They need a place to send a unit and trust it comes back ready. That is what our turnover ticket service is built for.

You send one ticket — the address, how to get in, what the unit needs and when it has to be ready. We schedule it, send one crew that cleans, repairs, patches, paints and finishes the floors, and close it out with photos of the finished unit. No calling a cleaner, then a painter, then a handyman. No driving across town to check.

It works for a brokerage prepping a listing, a management company turning a rental between tenants, or an investor getting a newly bought property ready to lease. The ticket is the same; the scope changes.

Photo proof

See the finished unit without driving out

Photo proof changes how you manage a spread-out portfolio. When a ticket closes, you get pictures of the finished rooms — kitchen, bathrooms, floors, walls. Leasing agents can review a unit from the office. Asset managers can show an owner exactly what they paid for. And if there is ever a question about the condition a unit was handed back in, there is a dated record.

Photos also keep us honest. A crew that knows its work will be photographed and reviewed does the corners, the baseboards and the inside of the oven. It is the same standard we hold on large jobs, like student-housing turns of 800+ units in a single turn, where finished units are photographed as they are signed off.

FAQ

Turnover ticket questions

What is a turnover ticket?

A turnover ticket is a single work request for one unit or property: the address, access details, the scope and the deadline. You send it, we schedule it, do the work with one crew and send completion photos when it is done.

What kind of work can one ticket cover?

Anything a unit needs between occupants or before a listing: deep cleaning, repairs, patch and paint, carpet extraction and floor care, trash-out, punch-list items and listing-prep cleans. If the unit needs a bigger renovation, we scope it separately under the same contract.

Do you provide photo proof?

Yes. Every completed ticket comes back with photos of the finished unit, so you can confirm the work, share it with an owner or use it for the listing without driving out to the property.

Can we set up a standing scope for repeat units?

Yes. Most real-estate and property-management clients agree a standard scope per unit type once. After that, each ticket only needs the address, access and deadline, and every unit is delivered to the same standard.

How do we get TPS Pro approved as a vendor?

We send a W-9 and a certificate of insurance naming your company before the first ticket. Our vendor packet has everything procurement teams usually ask for in one place.

Which areas do you cover?

Albany and the Capital Region, including Troy, Schenectady, Saratoga Springs, Clifton Park, Cohoes, Ballston Spa and Glens Falls. Our crews also take work in NYC, Westchester, New Jersey and Florida for existing clients.
