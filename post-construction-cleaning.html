How it works

The three phases of post-construction cleaning

A finished build isn't cleaned in a single pass. We break post-construction cleanup into three distinct phases so the space is safe to work in, detailed to a finish, and ready the moment you hand over the keys.

1 · Rough clean

Done during or right after the major work. We clear construction debris, bag out trash, pull stickers and packaging, and knock down the heavy drywall dust and jobsite grime so the trades that follow have a clean, safe surface to finish on. This is the phase that keeps a project moving instead of tripping over its own mess.

2 · Final clean

The detailed pass. We wipe down every surface, detail fixtures, vents, and hardware, clean interior glass, window frames, and tracks, and scrub and finish the floors. Baseboards, frames, sills, and corners all get worked by hand. This is the phase that turns a built-out shell into a space that photographs and shows well.

3 · Touch-up clean

The last look before handoff or occupancy. Right before your walkthrough, inspection, or move-in date, we return to remove any settled dust, fingerprints, and last-minute debris left by final trades. The space is left genuinely handoff-ready on the date you committed to your client or tenant.

Scope of work

What we remove & detail

Construction leaves behind residue that ordinary cleaning never touches. Our crews are equipped for the full range of post-build cleanup:

Construction dust on every surface Adhesive, stickers & label residue Paint overspray & splatter Fine drywall & joint-compound dust Debris collection & haul-out Windows, frames & track cleaning Fixture, vent & hardware detailing Floor stripping & finishing

The single biggest challenge after a build is dust. Sanding drywall and cutting materials throws fine particulate into the air that settles into every seam, on top of every door frame, inside light fixtures, and across every horizontal surface long after the trades have gone. A quick sweep just resettles it. We work top-down and detail by hand so the dust is actually removed, not pushed around — the difference between a space that looks clean for an hour and one that stays clean for the walkthrough.

Beyond dust, we deal with the marks a finished build always leaves: adhesive and stickers on new windows and appliances, paint overspray on glass and hardware, and joint-compound haze on floors. We haul out the leftover debris, clean glass and window tracks, detail vents and fixtures, and strip and finish flooring so the surfaces read as new. Scope is always matched to your project, so you only pay for the phases and detail your handoff actually needs.

FAQ

Post-construction cleaning questions

What is post-construction cleaning?

Post-construction cleaning is the specialized cleanup that takes a newly built or renovated space from a debris-filled jobsite to a finished, occupiable space. It removes construction dust, debris, adhesive, and residue that ordinary cleaning isn't equipped to handle, and details surfaces, glass, fixtures, and floors so the space is ready for handoff, inspection, or move-in.

What are the phases of post-construction cleaning?

There are three: the rough clean during or right after major work (debris, trash, heavy dust), the final clean that details surfaces, fixtures, glass, and floors, and the touch-up clean right before handoff or occupancy that removes any settled dust and last-minute debris. Not every project needs all three — we match the phases to your timeline.

How is post-construction cleaning priced?

Every job is quoted based on the size of the space and the scope of work — square footage, the phases you need, and how much detail the handoff requires. We don't use one-size-fits-all pricing because no two builds leave the same mess. The estimate is free, and we'll give you a real number for your specific project.

How soon can you clean after construction?

We mobilize quickly and schedule around your build. Because we run 24/7 — including overnight and weekend turnarounds — we can often clean on the exact day you need the space ready, even when that means working after the final trade walks off late in the day.

Do you haul away construction debris?

Yes. Debris collection and haul-out are part of the work — we bag out trash, clear leftover material, and coordinate removal so the space is genuinely cleared, not just swept.

Do you clean windows and vents?

Yes. We clean interior windows, frames, and tracks, and we detail vents, light fixtures, and hardware as part of the final clean. These are exactly the spots where construction dust hides and a quick clean misses.

Are you insured?

Yes — TPS Pro is bonded and insured, and we provide a certificate of insurance before day one so your documentation is in place before we mobilize.

Do you also do the renovation or buildout itself?

Yes. We run renovations and construction in-house — buildouts, paint, flooring, and drywall — so the same company that builds your space can also clean it. One company, one contract, from demo to done.
