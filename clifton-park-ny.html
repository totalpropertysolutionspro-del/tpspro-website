Local knowledge

Southern Saratoga County's commercial engine

Clifton Park sits at the heart of southern Saratoga County, with the Northway running through town and Route 9 and Route 146 carrying most of its commercial activity. The corridors are lined with professional office parks, medical and dental offices, retail plazas, restaurants and service businesses — the kind of buildings that need reliable, recurring cleaning rather than a one-time visit.

Behind the commercial strips are established residential neighborhoods, townhouse and condo communities, and apartment complexes. Many are run by HOA boards or management companies that need common areas kept up week after week and units turned quickly when residents move.

Route 9 & Clifton Country Road

Retail centers, restaurants and office buildings around the town's main commercial crossroads. After-hours janitorial, floor care and entryway upkeep.

Route 146 corridor
