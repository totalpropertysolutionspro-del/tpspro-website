Where UAlbany students live

Off-campus Albany, block by block

UAlbany has two campuses — uptown on Washington Avenue and downtown on Western Avenue — and students spread across the neighborhoods between and around them. Each area turns differently.

Pine Hills

Albany's main off-campus student district, between Western and Madison Avenues on streets like Ontario, Partridge, Quail and South Main. Mostly two- and three-family houses owned by landlords with anywhere from one to dozens of doors — and nearly all of them turn in the same weeks.

Uptown & Washington Avenue

Purpose-built student communities and larger apartment complexes near the uptown campus, many leased by the bed. High unit counts, shared suites, amenity spaces and a hard move-in date.

Downtown campus & Western Avenue

Apartments and houses near the downtown campus and the edge of Washington Park. A mix of undergrad and graduate renters, older buildings and tight parking.

Center Square & Hudson/Park

Walk-ups and brownstone apartments that attract upperclassmen and grad students. Older plaster, hardwood and narrow stairwells that need a careful crew.

Two kinds of UAlbany turn

Scattered houses or one big building — we plan for both

Scattered-site turns in Pine Hills are a routing problem as much as a cleaning problem. Forty houses on twelve streets means forty sets of keys, forty trash pile-ups on forty porches, and on-street parking that disappears when everyone moves out on the same weekend. We map the addresses, group them by block, and send crews that can clean, repair, paint and patch in a single visit so you are not coordinating a cleaner, a painter and a handyman at every door.

Purpose-built communities near the uptown campus are a sequencing problem. Hundreds of beds, shared bathrooms and kitchens, study lounges, fitness rooms and hallways all need to be finished before the first resident arrives. We work floor by floor, room by room, inspect each unit against your checklist, and flag damage beyond normal wear as we find it so your team can document it for deposits before the repair is made.

Either way you get one crew and one point of contact who knows how many units are done, how many are in progress, and what is left before move-in.

The UAlbany calendar

How a turn season runs around UAlbany

Exact dates change every year, so we build the schedule from your lease-end and move-in dates. The pattern stays the same.

Winter: walk and book

January through March we walk your buildings, count units, agree on scope and reserve crew capacity. Peak weeks can fill as early as February.

Spring: plan the waves

We lock a unit list, order paint and materials, confirm key access and set the order of work by lease-end date.

May: move-out wave

After spring finals, students leave. We start with the units that empty first — trash-out, deep clean, and a damage list for your team.

June–July: repair and paint

Repairs, paint and patch on walls, ceilings and doors, carpet and floor care, and any unit that turned over mid-summer.

August: finish and move-in

Final cleans, hallways, stairwells and common areas, and a punch list so every unit is ready before late-August move-in.

UAlbany turnover FAQ

Questions from UAlbany-area operators

Do you work for the University at Albany?

No. We do not hold contracts with UAlbany. Our clients are the private property managers, landlords and student-housing operators who own and run off-campus housing near the uptown and downtown campuses and in Pine Hills.

When should we book a UAlbany-area turn?

As early as you can. Turn season runs from May through August, and the peak weeks after spring finals and before late-August move-in book up first, sometimes as early as February. Booking early lets us walk the units, order paint and lock in crew size.

Can you handle dozens of scattered Pine Hills houses instead of one building?

Yes. We route crews street by street, track keys and lockboxes for every address, and sequence the work so each house gets cleaned, repaired, painted and patched in one visit wherever possible, rather than sending a different vendor for each task.

What does a TPS Pro student turnover include?

A full-service turn: deep cleaning of every room, kitchens and bathrooms, repairs, paint and patch on walls, ceilings and doors, carpet and floor care, and cleaning of hallways, stairwells and other common areas. One crew does all of it.

How big a turn can you take on?

We have turned more than 800 units in a single turn and more than 5,000 units to date. Whether you have six houses in Pine Hills or a full purpose-built community near the uptown campus, we size the crew to your unit count and deadline.
