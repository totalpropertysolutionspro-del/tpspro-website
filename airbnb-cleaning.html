Why hosts switch

Consistency is what reviews are made of

Guests rate cleanliness on every stay, and it's the category that sinks listings fastest. One missed hair in a shower or a sticky counter can undo a run of good reviews. Most turnover problems aren't about effort — they're about inconsistency: a different cleaner every week, no checklist, and nobody noticing the coffee pods ran out.

We fix that with a written checklist per property, the same crew on your listings wherever possible, and a quick message after each turnover. If something's wrong — damage, a missing item, a broken appliance — you hear about it before the next guest does. And because TPS Pro also handles property maintenance, a dripping faucet or a broken blind can be fixed by the same company instead of waiting on a separate handyman.

The details

Linens, supplies and access

Linens. Most hosts keep two or three sets per bed on site. We strip, make the beds with a clean set, and bag used linens for your laundry service or wash them on site where the property has the machines and the turnover window allows. Stained or torn items get set aside and flagged so you can replace them.

Supplies. Toilet paper, paper towels, soap, dish liquid, coffee, trash bags — we restock from your closet on every visit and keep a simple count. When something drops low, you get a message with enough lead time to reorder.

Access. Lockbox and smart-lock codes are kept with the crew assigned to your property, and we confirm the door is locked and the code reset (if you rotate codes) when we leave.

Staging. Send us your listing photos and we stage to match: pillows, throws, towel folds, welcome notes. Guests should walk into the place they booked, not an approximation of it.

FAQ

Airbnb cleaning questions, answered

How do I schedule recurring Airbnb turnovers with TPS Pro?

We set up a recurring account. You share your booking calendar (Airbnb, VRBO or an iCal link) and we schedule turnovers around your checkout and check-in times. If your bookings are irregular, you can also message us as bookings come in. Call (518) 948-7156 to get set up.

Can you handle same-day turnovers?

Yes. Most short-term rental turnovers are same-day, between an 11 a.m. checkout and a 3 or 4 p.m. check-in. We schedule crews around those windows. During peak weeks, book early so we can plan crew routes across your listings.

Do you restock supplies?

Yes. Restocking from your on-site supply closet is part of every turnover, and we tell you when anything is running low so you can reorder before it runs out.

What happens if you find damage after a guest checks out?

We photograph it and message you before the next check-in, so you have what you need to file a claim within the platform's window and decide whether the next stay can go ahead.

Do you work with property managers who run multiple listings?

Yes. We work with hosts and short-term rental managers with several listings, using one schedule and one point of contact, and the same crew on the same properties wherever possible.

What areas do you cover?

Saratoga Springs, Albany, Clifton Park, Malta, Troy, Schenectady, Ballston Spa and Glens Falls, plus surrounding Capital Region communities. For the Lake George area, contact us early in the season to confirm availability.
