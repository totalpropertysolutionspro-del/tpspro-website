Local knowledge

From Monument Square to the waterfront

Troy is one of the Capital Region's most distinctive cities: a compact nineteenth-century downtown with cast-iron storefronts and brownstone blocks, a working Hudson River waterfront, and a hill of college buildings rising above it. Historic architecture and active redevelopment sit side by side, and that shapes how buildings here need to be cleaned and maintained.

Downtown around Monument Square, River Street and Third Street has filled with restaurants, creative offices, retail and upper-floor apartments. Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute sits on the hill east of downtown, Russell Sage College's campus is in the heart of downtown, and Hudson Valley Community College is in South Troy — so the city carries a large, steady student rental market that turns every summer.

Downtown & Monument Square

Offices, restaurants and mixed-use buildings near the Troy Savings Bank Music Hall and the Saturday farmers market. After-hours janitorial and floor care around busy weekends.

RPI hill & the east side

Houses and small apartment buildings off Burdett Avenue, around 15th Street and toward Hoosick Street, rented mostly to RPI students. Turned every summer on tight windows.

South Troy & the Vandenburgh corridor

Rental housing near Hudson Valley Community College and older industrial buildings along the river. Make-ready, maintenance and renovation cleanup.

Lansingburgh & North Troy

Two-family houses and apartment buildings with steady tenant turnover. Make-ready cleaning, paint and patch, and repair work orders for landlords with several addresses.

Troy FAQ

Questions about service in Troy

Do you clean commercial buildings in downtown Troy?

Yes. We provide nightly and weekly janitorial for offices, restaurants, retail and mixed-use buildings around Monument Square, River Street, Broadway and Third Street. Most downtown work happens after hours, and we follow each building's access and key rules.

Can you turn off-campus housing near RPI, Russell Sage and Hudson Valley Community College?

Yes. We turn student and rental units across Troy for property managers and student-housing operators, from houses and walk-ups near RPI to apartments downtown and in South Troy. Each unit gets a deep clean, repairs, paint and patch, and the common areas are finished by the same crew.

Do you work on older Troy buildings with plaster, tin ceilings and hardwood floors?

Yes. Much of Troy's housing and commercial stock is nineteenth-century. We patch plaster and drywall, paint to match, and clean and refinish hardwood floors with methods that suit older materials, and we handle post-construction cleaning when those buildings are renovated.

Is Troy outside your normal service area?

No. Troy is minutes from our Albany base, and it is part of our regular Capital Region coverage. Crews are on call 24/7 for Troy properties, including emergency maintenance and after-hours cleaning.
