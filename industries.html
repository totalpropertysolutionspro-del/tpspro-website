Student housing

Turn season is a fixed deadline: every unit cleaned, repaired, painted and move-in ready before keys go out. We've turned 800+ units in a single turn and 5,000+ to date for operators such as PeakMade Real Estate, College Suites, Auden Albany, Block 75 and The Coda & Mayflower in Syracuse. We work around UAlbany, RPI, Skidmore, Union College and Hudson Valley CC, and peak windows book as early as February.

Student turnovers · UAlbany · RPI & Troy · Skidmore · Union College

Multifamily & apartments

Apartment operators need units back on the market fast and common areas that sell the building on every tour. We handle make-readies with repairs and paint in the same visit, recurring hallway and lobby cleaning, and floor care for corridors and amenity spaces. Operators such as Rosenblum Companies, Beacon Communities and WJW Management rely on that one-crew model.

Make-ready cleaning · Property maintenance · Floor care

Commercial offices

Office tenants notice the restrooms, the break room and the trash — every night. We run evening and overnight janitorial on a written task list, with periodic floor and carpet care built into the schedule instead of bolted on. Facility teams at firms such as Cushman & Wakefield, AECOM and FirstLight Fiber have used TPS Pro.

Commercial cleaning · Office cleaning · Janitorial services

Medical offices

Exam rooms, waiting areas and restrooms need consistent disinfection of high-touch surfaces and a crew that follows your practice's protocols. We clean after hours so patient schedules aren't disrupted, and keep the same crew on your building so the routine doesn't drift.

Medical office cleaning · Janitorial services

Schools & universities

Schools need heavy-traffic cleaning during the year and a big reset when students leave. We've served SUNY, Albany City School District and East Greenbush CSD, with summer floor stripping and refinishing, classroom and hallway deep cleans, and repairs between terms.

Floor care · Janitorial services · Facility maintenance

Retail

Retail floors take a beating and customers judge the store by them. We scrub, burnish and strip and wax sales floors, clean restrooms and back-of-house, and schedule around store hours. Retail clients include Golub Corporation and Curaleaf.

Retail floor care · Commercial cleaning

Warehouses & logistics

Distribution buildings run around the clock, so cleaning has to fit between shifts. We use ride-on scrubbers on large floors and handle break rooms, locker rooms, offices and restrooms. We've worked for logistics and industrial names such as FedEx, UPS, CSX and Sonoco.

Janitorial services · Industrial floor care · Facility maintenance

HOA & condo communities

Boards and managing agents want lobbies, hallways, stairwells and amenity rooms kept to one standard, plus someone to call when something breaks. We combine recurring common-area cleaning with maintenance and repairs under one contract.

HOA & condo cleaning · Property maintenance

General contractors & new construction

GCs need rough, final and touch-up cleans timed to inspections and owner walkthroughs, and a sub who can also fix punch-list paint and drywall. We deliver turnover-ready spaces and send the COI and W-9 fast so we're on site when you need us.

For general contractors · Post-construction cleaning · Renovation cleaning

Real estate & property managers

Listings, vacancies and portfolio turnovers need one vendor who can take a ticket and close it — clean, repair, paint and photo-ready. Property managers such as Belmont Management, Morrison Management, DMG Investments and Amicus Properties use us across their buildings.

Real-estate turnovers · Property management · Cleaning for property managers
