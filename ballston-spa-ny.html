Local knowledge

The county seat and the Malta corridor

Ballston Spa is the seat of Saratoga County. The county's municipal center and courts are here, alongside a compact historic village of brick storefronts, professional offices, restaurants and small shops. Every summer the Saratoga County Fair brings crowds to the fairgrounds on the edge of the village.

The village sits between Albany and Saratoga Springs, and just to the south is Malta and the Luther Forest Technology Campus, home to the GlobalFoundries semiconductor plant. That corridor has driven years of new commercial buildings, apartments and housing developments — and steady demand for construction cleanup, janitorial and make-ready work.

Village center

Brick storefronts, offices and restaurants on Front Street and Milton Avenue. Janitorial scheduled around business hours.

County offices area

Law firms, title companies and professional practices that cluster near the county buildings. Consistent, discreet office cleaning.

Malta & Luther Forest corridor
