What we provide

Everything procurement asks for

These are the documents property managers, facility teams and general contractors typically need before a new vendor can start. Ask for all of them or only the ones you need.

W-9

A current, signed IRS Form W-9 for Total Property Solutions Pro LLC, so accounts payable can set us up and issue payment.

Certificate of insurance

A COI issued to your company as certificate holder. We can name your company — and your owner or management entity — as additional insured on request.

Business entity information

Legal name, business address, remit-to details and the contacts your vendor master file needs, provided in writing.

Emergency contacts

A 24/7 dispatch number — (518) 948-7156 — plus named contacts for scheduling, billing and escalation.

Service agreement & scope

A written service agreement or scope document for the work — frequencies, task lists, turn schedules or project phases — ready for your review or your own contract template.

Your forms, completed

Vendor setup forms, supplier questionnaires or compliance-platform invitations: send them over and we fill them out.

Why it matters

Onboarding shouldn't hold up the work

Most delays in bringing on a cleaning or maintenance vendor aren't about the work. They're about paperwork: a COI with the wrong certificate holder, a W-9 that never arrives, a vendor form that sits unanswered in someone's inbox. We treat onboarding as part of the job. The same team that schedules your crew handles your paperwork, so questions get answered by someone who knows the scope.

That matters most when timing is tight. Student-housing operators often add vendors weeks before turn season. General contractors need a COI on file before a sub steps on site. Facility managers switching janitorial providers want the new crew running the night the old contract ends. Ask for the packet early and you won't be waiting on it later.

One contract also means one onboarding. Because TPS Pro handles cleaning, turnovers, floor care, repairs, paint and renovations, one vendor setup can cover work that would otherwise take four or five separate vendors — and four or five sets of paperwork.

Questions

Vendor onboarding FAQ

How fast can you send a W-9 and certificate of insurance?

Typically within one business day of your request. If the COI needs specific additional-insured wording or a project address, include it in the request so it goes out right the first time.

Can you name our company as additional insured?

Yes, on request. Tell us the exact legal names to list — your company, the property owner or the management entity — and the certificate-holder address.

Will you complete our vendor setup forms or compliance-portal invitation?

Yes. Send the form or the invitation to bookings@totalpropertysolution.net and we complete it. If something needs a signature or a document we haven't sent yet, we'll follow up directly.

Who do we call after hours once you're set up?

Our 24/7 line: (518) 948-7156. Emergencies such as leaks, floods and urgent cleanups are answered any hour, every day of the year.

Do you need a signed contract before we onboard you?

No. Many clients onboard us first so we're ready when the work is. When you're ready to book, we provide a written scope or service agreement, or work from your own contract template.
