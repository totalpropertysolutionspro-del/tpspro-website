Pricing & getting started

Quotes, insurance & scheduling

How much does commercial cleaning cost in Albany NY?

Every job is quoted from your scope, so the number reflects your building rather than a generic rate card. Price depends on square footage, how often we clean, the mix of floors, restrooms and break rooms, and add-ons like floor care or day porter hours. Student turnovers are quoted per unit based on condition and scope, and post-construction cleans are quoted per project and phase. Request a free quote and we'll come back with a written scope and a number, usually within 24 hours.

Are you insured and bonded?

Yes. TPS Pro is bonded and insured, and we send a certificate of insurance and a W-9 before your first service. If your company needs to be named as additional insured, tell us when you request the certificate. Our vendor packet page lists everything we provide to procurement teams.

How quickly can you start cleaning?

Once the scope is agreed, recurring cleaning can usually start soon after the walkthrough, and we give you a start date with the quote. Emergencies are answered 24/7 at (518) 948-7156. For large jobs like student-housing turn season or phased post-construction work, book early so we can reserve crew coverage. Peak turn windows book as early as February.

Do you use subcontractors?

Our own crews do the work. That's the point of TPS Pro: one experienced crew instead of a dozen inexperienced vendors, with one point of contact who knows your building and your scope.

Our services

Turnovers, repairs & construction cleaning

Do you do student housing turnover cleaning?

Yes — student housing turnover is one of our specialties. During turn season (May through August) we have turned 800+ units in a single turn across the Capital Region, including Albany, Troy, Schenectady and Saratoga Springs, with 5,000+ units turned to date. We don't just clean: we repair, paint, patch walls, ceilings and doors, and turn the hallways and common areas too — one experienced crew for the whole building. Learn more about student turnover cleaning.

Do you handle repairs and painting, or only cleaning?

Both. Our crews handle drywall patching, painting, minor repairs, drains and general property maintenance, plus larger renovations and buildouts. Bundling them with cleaning is how we keep one contract for the whole building. See property maintenance and renovations.

Do you offer floor care?

Yes. We strip and wax, scrub, burnish and refinish hard floors, and run hot-water extraction on carpet — as a one-time job, a turnover add-on or a recurring program. Learn more about floor care.

Do you offer Airbnb and short-term rental cleaning?

Yes. We provide Airbnb and short-term rental turnover cleaning, including linen resets, restocks and guest-ready inspections, for hosts across Albany, Saratoga Springs and the wider Capital Region. Learn more about Airbnb cleaning services.

Can you handle post-construction cleaning?

Absolutely. Post-construction cleanup is one of our most requested services. We handle drywall dust, debris removal, windows and the final polish to get your space handoff-ready, and we can fix punch-list paint and drywall items in the same visit. We work directly with general contractors, developers and property managers. Learn more about post-construction cleaning or see our page for general contractors.
