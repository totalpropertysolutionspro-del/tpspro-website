How a project runs

From demo to move-in ready

Every renovation and buildout we run in Albany & the Capital Region follows the same clear path — so you always know what's happening, who's on site, and what comes next. One company owns the whole sequence, from the first walkthrough to the final spotless handoff.

1 · Walkthrough & estimate

We meet you on site, walk the space, and map the scope together — what stays, what goes, and what the finished space needs to do. You get a real, quote-based estimate built around your project, not a guess over the phone.

2 · Demo & prep

Once you're ready, our crew handles demolition, tear-out, and debris removal, then preps the space — protecting floors, framing, and surfaces so the build goes clean. A certificate of insurance is on file before anyone sets foot on site.

3 · The build

This is the heart of the job: drywall and patching, flooring and vinyl, interior and exterior painting, and fixtures set and finished. The same accountable crew carries the work through, so quality and schedule stay under one roof.

4 · Punch-list

We walk the space with you and build a punch-list of every last detail — touch-ups, adjustments, and finish work. Nothing gets called done until each item is checked off and the space meets the standard we promised.

5 · Post-construction clean

Because we clean too, the same team follows the build with a full in-house post-construction cleaning — drywall dust, debris, windows, and final polish — so the space is handed over ready to use, not ready to clean.

6 · Final handoff

We do a final walk with you, confirm the punch-list is closed, and hand over a finished, move-in ready space. And since we also maintain and turn properties, we're one call away long after the keys change hands.

Scope of work

What we build & renovate

TPS Pro handles interior renovation and light construction across commercial and residential property alike. Whether it's a tenant or office buildout, a full apartment renovation, or an interior remodel, the same in-house crew delivers the work — and finishes it with our own post-construction cleaning.

On the commercial side, we build out tenant and office spaces from bare shell or existing footprint — reconfiguring layouts, hanging and patching drywall, laying flooring and vinyl, and painting interiors and exteriors so the space is ready for occupancy. On the residential side, we renovate apartments and individual units and take on interior remodels, from single rooms to full unit refreshes between tenants.

Across both, the core trades stay the same: demolition and debris removal to clear the way, drywall and patching to make walls right, flooring and vinyl installation, and interior and exterior painting to finish. Every project closes with punch-list completion and a thorough in-house post-construction clean — no separate vendor, no gap between "built" and "clean."
