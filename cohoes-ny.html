Local knowledge

From the mills to the Falls

Cohoes has a character unlike any other city in the Capital Region. The Spindle City takes its name from its nineteenth-century textile industry, and the huge brick mills along the Mohawk River — Harmony Mills above all — are now being converted into apartments, lofts and mixed-use space. The Cohoes Falls, one of the largest waterfalls in the state, draws visitors to the north end of town.

Mill conversions are some of the most demanding cleaning projects in the region: tall ceilings, exposed beams and brick, long corridors, freight elevators and a lot of fine construction dust. Once they open, the same buildings need ongoing common-area cleaning and quick apartment turns. Cohoes also sits just across the river from Troy and a short drive from Albany, and many renters work or study in both.

Harmony Hill & the mill district

Large converted mill complexes with loft apartments. Post-construction cleans, common-area janitorial and loft turnovers.

Downtown & Remsen Street

Storefronts, offices and restaurants near the Cohoes Music Hall. Nightly or weekly janitorial and floor care.

Cohoes Falls & the north end

Mixed residential streets near the Falls and the Mohawk. Make-ready and maintenance for landlords with several addresses.

Van Schaick Island & nearby towns

Residential rentals on the island and in neighboring Waterford, Watervliet and Latham, on the same route.

Yes. We clean large converted mill buildings with high ceilings, exposed brick and industrial finishes, both after construction and on a recurring basis for common areas, and we turn loft apartments between tenants.

Yes. We work with general contractors and developers on renovations and new units, doing the rough, final and touch-up cleans — dust on every ledge, debris, windows, floors and fixtures — so the project can pass inspection and hand over.

Do you handle make-ready for Cohoes apartments?

Yes. We turn apartments between tenants with a deep clean, repairs, paint and patch, and carpet or floor care, for landlords with one building or many.

Do you provide janitorial for downtown Cohoes businesses?

Yes. We clean offices, retail and small commercial spaces along Remsen Street and the rest of downtown on nightly or weekly schedules.
