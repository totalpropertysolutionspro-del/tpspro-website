Local knowledge

Racing season, Skidmore and downtown

Saratoga Springs is unlike any other city in upstate New York. Every summer the Saratoga Race Course meet, which runs from July into Labor Day weekend, fills hotels and short-term rentals and puts pressure on every restaurant and shop downtown. Concerts at the Saratoga Performing Arts Center in the state park add to it. Then the calendar turns back to a steady year-round market of offices, medical practices and retail.

Skidmore College, just north of downtown, adds a student rental market that turns after graduation — often in the same weeks that owners are getting properties ready for summer guests. Some owners lease to students for the school year and rent to visitors in summer, which means two different kinds of turnover in the same property. We plan both.

Broadway & downtown

Offices, restaurants, bars, galleries and boutiques from Congress Park up Broadway. Late closes mean overnight janitorial and early-morning floor care.

Union Avenue & the track

Homes and apartments near the Race Course that rent by the week or weekend in summer. Rapid, repeatable turnovers with linens and restock.

North Broadway & Skidmore

Off-campus student houses and small apartment buildings near campus. Full turnovers after graduation, before summer or fall tenants arrive.

West Side, East Side & Route 50

Neighborhood rentals and condos, medical offices near Saratoga Hospital, and retail and office space along Route 50 toward Wilton.
