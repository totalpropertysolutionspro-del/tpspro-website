Who this is for

Make-ready for apartment communities and property managers

Every day a vacant unit sits waiting on a cleaner, then a painter, then a handyman, is a day of rent you do not get back. The work itself is not complicated. The coordination is. Three vendors means three schedules, three invoices and at least one of them waiting on another before they can start.

TPS Pro runs make-ready as a single job. One crew opens the unit, does the repairs, patches and paints, cleans top to bottom, extracts the carpet or finishes the hard floors, and checks the result against your punch list. You get one number to call, one invoice and one company responsible for the unit being ready on the date you gave us.

This page is for multifamily owners, apartment communities and property management companies with ongoing turnover. If you run student housing with a summer turn, see our student housing turnover page. If you just want to understand the process, our make-ready cleaning guide walks through it step by step.

How it works

From vacancy to rent-ready in four steps

Send the unit

Email or call with the address, unit, access and the date it has to be ready. Repeat clients just send the unit — your scope is on file.

Assess and confirm

We walk the unit, note anything outside the standard scope, and confirm before extra work starts. No surprise invoices.

Repair, paint, then clean

Work runs in the right order: repairs and patching first, paint next, then the deep clean and floors so dust from repairs never lands on a finished unit.

Punch and hand back

We check the unit against the punch list, close any misses and hand it back ready to show, with completion photos when you want them.

Why one crew

Done in the right order, the first time

Make-ready is one of those jobs where the order of work matters as much as the work itself. Clean a unit before the drywall is patched and you will clean it twice. Paint before the repairs are finished and you will paint twice. Because one crew does every trade, we sequence the unit properly the first time.

We also turn units at the volume multifamily portfolios actually generate. That can mean a steady stream of single vacancies through the year, or a whole building of lease-ends in the same few weeks. Our crews have turned 800+ units in a single student-housing turn, so a week of apartment lease-ends does not stretch us. Where a portfolio spans towns, one contract covers Albany, Troy, Schenectady, Clifton Park and Saratoga Springs.

Paperwork is simple. We are bonded and insured, and send a W-9 and certificate of insurance before day one. Most property management companies set us up once through our vendor packet and then send units by email or text as they vacate.

FAQ

Make-ready questions, answered

What is a make-ready?

A make-ready is everything a vacant apartment needs between one lease and the next so it can be shown, inspected and moved into: a deep clean, repairs, paint and patch, floor care and a final check against a punch list. We handle all of it with one crew.

Is make-ready the same as a move-out clean?

No. A move-out clean only covers cleaning. A make-ready also covers the repairs, wall patching, painting, carpet extraction and punch-list items that stop a unit from being rent-ready. That is the difference between a unit that looks clean and a unit you can lease.

Can you handle single units as well as whole buildings?

Yes. We turn one-off vacancies for small landlords and run scheduled make-readies across entire apartment communities. For portfolios with steady turnover, we set up a standing scope so each ticket is quick to send and consistent to deliver.

How do we send you a unit?

Email bookings@totalpropertysolution.net or call (518) 948-7156 with the address, unit number, access details and the date the unit needs to be ready. For repeat clients we keep your standard scope on file, so the unit and the deadline are usually all we need.

How is make-ready priced?

Every property is quoted after a walkthrough, based on unit size, condition and the scope you want — cleaning only, or cleaning plus repairs and paint. For portfolios, we agree on a standing scope per unit type so every ticket is priced the same way.

Are you insured, and can you get set up as a vendor?

Yes. TPS Pro is bonded and insured. We send a W-9 and a certificate of insurance before day one, naming your company as certificate holder, so your accounting and compliance teams have what they need before the first unit.
