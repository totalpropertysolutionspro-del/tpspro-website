Local knowledge

One of upstate New York's fastest-growing counties

Saratoga County has been one of the fastest-growing counties in upstate New York for years. New homes and apartments keep rising in Malta, Clifton Park, Halfmoon and Wilton; commercial development follows along Route 9, Route 50 and the Northway exits; and the Luther Forest Technology Campus in Malta has brought a wave of supporting businesses.

Add the Skidmore College rental market, a summer short-term rental boom during the Saratoga race meet, and the lake and village communities further out, and you get a county where property needs change from town to town and month to month. We have built routes across all of it, not just the city of Saratoga Springs.
